THINKING
Enter position without reservation only after my edge is truly validated and my risk-stop is set in stone
The most critical rule is to exit a losing position immediately, at my preset stop or sooner. PERIOD
Closing a losing position immediately: Stop the bleeding, Free the rational mind, and Preserve capital
Cutting my losers quickly is a good business investment. Not doing so is suicidal wishful thinking
Beware of the SUNK COST FALLACY. Always consider future predicaments, not past investments
One blown stop is variance, not a lost edge - could just be too early - re-assess only after a streak
Several blown off stops could mean that I may have lost my edge, could be time to rethink my strategy
Always beneficial to reconsider my approach - and objectively re-evaluate my bias and convictions
It's essential to stay flexible and responsive - and adapt to continuously changing market conditions
Trimming is critical for consistent financial success - one win at the time - as trading is a MARATHON
Not trimmming incrementally because of greed - the lure of more - will inevitably lead to losses
Big bags can happen but not very often - Aim at incremental gains rather than shooting for the stars
